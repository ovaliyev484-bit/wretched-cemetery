Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the «Vault Calculator» Android app (the "App") handles and protects user data. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. About the App

Vault Calculator looks and works like a regular calculator, but contains a secure vault that opens with a secret PIN. Users can hide their photos, videos, and other files inside the vault.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or location.
  • No registration or account is required.
  • Files you add to the vault, your PIN, and your settings are stored only on your device.

3. Data Storage

All files and settings are stored in the App's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or sent over the internet. If you uninstall the App, vault data may be deleted, so please back up important files beforehand.

4. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions needed to work:

  • Storage / photos and videos (media) access — to add files to the vault and restore them.
  • Camera (if used) — to capture photos or videos directly into the vault.
  • Biometric authentication (if used) — to unlock the vault with fingerprint or face. Biometric data is handled by the Android system; the App cannot see or store it.

Data accessed through these permissions never leaves your device.

5. Advertising and Analytics

If the App uses third-party services (for example, Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics), they may collect an advertising identifier and general device information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Your vault files are never sent to such services.

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, keeping your PIN secret and securing your device (screen lock) is your responsibility. If you forget your PIN, it may not be possible to recover it.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your Rights

Since your data stays on your device, you are in full control: you can export your files from the vault or delete everything by uninstalling the App.
